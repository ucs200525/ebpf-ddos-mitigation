> **Design history.** Version 2. Version 1 (test seeds 1001-1005, archived in data/results/mitigation_results_v1.json) used absolute-volume scorer features, unbalanced scorer training and a <=2-packet spoofing trigger; inspecting its results showed legitimate users of small networks being blocked and the spoofing fallback never firing. The scorer features, training sampling and spoofing trigger were revised, and seeds 1001-1005 were retired: these results use unseen seeds 2001-2005.
