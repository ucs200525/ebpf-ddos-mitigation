# Figures

Generated by `scripts/make_figures.py`. Captions are suggestions for the report.

- **fig01_confusion_matrix.png** — Confusion matrix of the Random Forest on 27,262 held-out windows from 300 independent synthetic runs (row-normalized percentages).
- **fig02_roc_curve.png** — ROC curve of the detection model on the held-out windows (seed 1007); right panel zooms into low false-positive rates.
- **fig03_per_scenario_detection.png** — Per-scenario window-level results on the held-out test runs: recall on attack scenarios (left, note the axis starts at 90%) and false-positive rate on benign scenarios (right).
- **fig04_detector_comparison.png** — Second-by-second replay of fresh synthetic runs (seed 3031) through the statistical-only and hybrid detectors. Higher is better for the left two groups, lower for the right two.
- **fig05_feature_importance.png** — Random Forest impurity-based feature importance for the 16 window-level features.
- **fig06_mitigation_tradeoff.png** — Attack traffic dropped versus legitimate traffic dropped for each mitigation policy ("realistic" setting, hybrid detector, mean ± std over 5 test seeds x 100 runs). Up-and-left is better. The light blue points are other P4 thresholds; the line is their Pareto front. Diamonds are published baselines: AHIF (Goldstein et al. 2008) and signature filtering (Dimolianis et al. 2021); the hollow SIG* gives the signature filter a perfect classifier and is an upper bound, not a deployable system.
- **fig07_mitigation_by_setting.png** — Mitigation policies under three traffic-realism settings (clean: no client churn, no pre-attack bot traffic; realistic: 10% churn, 30% of bots active beforehand; hard: 20% / 60%). Bars are means, whiskers ± std over the test seeds. Exact values are in RESULTS_TABLES.md.
- **fig08_mitigation_by_scenario.png** — Current rule (P0) versus proposed source scoring (P4) per scenario group ("realistic" setting, mean over test seeds). Benign groups contain no attack packets, so only legitimate loss applies there.
- **fig09_example_timeline.png** — One "udp_flood" test run (realistic setting): offered vs admitted attack traffic (top) and legitimate traffic (bottom) under three policies. Gray bands mark seconds the detector flagged.
- **fig10_source_scorer_coefficients.png** — Logistic-regression coefficients of the per-source scorer (features standardized). Trained on tuning seed 900 only.
- **fig11_trace_driven.png** — Trace-driven evaluation: real legitimate traffic from MAWI samplepoint-F 200803181400 (first 180 s) toward 20 busy destinations, with injected attacks; mean ± std over 5 injection seeds x 40 runs. Nothing was tuned on the trace. SIG* (hatched) uses a perfect signature classifier and is an upper bound.
- **fig12_safety_scenarios.png** — Legitimate traffic dropped in the safety scenarios (synthetic, realistic setting, mean ± std over 5 seeds x 40 runs per scenario; no run used for tuning).
